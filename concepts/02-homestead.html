A builder who knows how projects really come together.

Jeremy began in construction and spent five to six years managing projects for an experienced builder. When the 2008 downturn reshaped the industry and his mentor retired, Jeremy built a business of his own—working across construction, landscaping, irrigation, and the practical needs that come with maintaining property. As the work grew, West Mountain Builders was established to take on larger residential and commercial projects without losing that hands-on approach.
