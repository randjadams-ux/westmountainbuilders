West Mountain Builders · Idaho

Six directions.
One solid foundation.

A set of distinct design explorations for a local builder specializing in new homes, additions, and remodeling.

01

Ridgeline Modern

Bold, architectural and confident. A premium direction led by dramatic scale and crisp typography.

 02

Idaho Homestead

Warm, grounded and neighborly. A modern farmhouse feel built around trust and craftsmanship.

 03

Blueprint

Process-forward and precise. A structured concept that makes planning and communication the hero.

 04

Field Notes

Editorial and tactile. A craftsman’s journal with strong photography, texture, and human detail.

 05

High Country

Dark, rugged and cinematic. Built to position West Mountain as the builder for ambitious Idaho homes.

 06

Clear Path

Bright, approachable and conversion-focused. A clean service site that makes the next step effortless.
